Step 1

Create your account

Sign up in the Mashora app with your email and phone number. We verify your number with a quick WhatsApp code.

  1. Open the app, tap Create account and choose Patient.
  2. Enter your full name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Pick your country — the dialing code fills in automatically — then type your phone number.
  4. Enter the 5-digit code we send to your WhatsApp. That's it — you're signed in.

Step 2

Find the right doctor

Browse verified doctors and narrow the list down until you find the right fit.

  • Search by doctor name or specialty.
  • Filter by specialty, price range and minimum rating.
  • Sort by soonest available, price or rating.
  • Open a profile to see the doctor's bio, rating, reviews and price per session — and read their CV.
  • Tap the heart to save a doctor to your favorites.

Step 3

Book your appointment

Choose a time that works for you. Every time is shown in your own local time zone.

  1. On the doctor's profile, scroll to Available times and tap a slot.
  2. Add an optional note for the doctor — your symptoms or the reason for your visit.
  3. Check the summary and tap Continue to payment.

Step 4

Pay securely

Stripe's secure payment page opens right inside the app.

  • Pay by card. Your card details go straight to Stripe — Mashora never sees or stores them.
  • You pay exactly the price shown on the doctor's profile. Nothing is added at checkout.
  • As soon as the payment goes through, your booking is confirmed and you get a notification.
  • Your doctor is notified about the new appointment at the same time.

Step 5

Prepare for your visit

Get more out of your consultation by sharing information with your doctor in advance.

  • Upload files to your booking — lab results, scans or X-rays as PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 20 MB each.
  • Message your doctor in the booking chat — it opens as soon as your booking is confirmed.
  • Before the call, find a quiet, well-lit place and check your internet connection.

Step 6

Join your video call

Your consultation happens inside the app — no links to copy and nothing else to install.

  1. We remind you 30 minutes before your appointment — and if your doctor joins first, we let you know they're waiting.
  2. At your start time, open the booking and tap Join video call. The button unlocks on its own — no need to refresh.
  3. Allow camera and microphone access the first time you join.
  4. Lost your connection? Tap Join again — the call stays open until 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

Step 7

After your visit

Your care doesn't end when the call does.

  • Medical report: your doctor writes up the diagnosis, medications and follow-up plan. You'll be notified when the PDF is ready in your medical records.
  • Rate your doctor from 1 to 5 stars with an optional comment — it helps other patients choose.
  • Follow-up questions: the booking chat stays open for 7 days after your appointment.
  • Need another visit? Book the same doctor again from your favorites.

FAQ

Patient questions

Is my appointment confirmed as soon as I book?

Your appointment is confirmed the moment your payment succeeds. Until then the slot is held for you for 15 minutes.

Which files can I share with my doctor?

PDF, JPG and PNG files up to 20 MB each — for example lab results, scans or X-rays. Upload them from your booking.

Why does my doctor see a different time than I do?

Your doctor may be in another country. Mashora shows every appointment in the local time of the phone you're using — it's the same moment for both of you.

Can I get my report again later?

Yes. All your reports stay in your medical records in the app, and you can download each one as a PDF at any time.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Payments are handled by Stripe, a global payments provider. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and never reach Mashora's servers.

Is Mashora for emergencies?

No. If you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department right away.
