Before you start

Where you'll work

Mashora gives you two tools that share the same account. Sign in to both with the same email and password.

The Mashora app

Your practice in your pocket — this is where consultations happen.

  • Video consultations with your patients
  • Availability, days off and your time zone
  • Bookings and patient chat
  • Verification documents and profile photo
  • Payout setup with Stripe

The web dashboard

Comfortable on a bigger screen — this is where you write reports.

  • Bookings with the patient's notes and files
  • Medical reports, generated as PDF
  • Weekly patterns, one-off slots and days off
  • Consultation fee and time zone
  • Patient chat and support
Video calls take place in the Mashora app, so keep it installed on the phone you'll consult from.

Step 1

Create your doctor account

Sign up from the Mashora app — it takes about two minutes.

  1. Open the app, tap Create account and choose Doctor.
  2. Enter your name, email, password, country and phone number.
  3. Add your doctor details: specialty (from the list), a short bio and your consultation fee.
  4. Confirm the 5-digit code we send to your WhatsApp.

Step 2

Get verified

Patients only see approved doctors. Getting approved takes one simple upload.

  1. In the app, open Profile Verification documents.
  2. Upload your CV and your specialty certificate — PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each.
  3. Connect your payout account (step 4) — it's required too. Our team reviews everything; your approval status is shown on your profile.
  4. Once you're approved, you can publish availability and receive bookings.

Step 3

Complete your profile

A complete profile helps patients choose you with confidence.

  • Profile photo — tap your photo in the app's Profile to add one. Patients see it on your profile.
  • Consultation fee — anywhere from $1 to $1,000 per session. Change it from the app's Profile or the dashboard's Profile page; new prices apply to new bookings only.
  • Time zone — set it from the dashboard's Profile page (one click detects your computer's time zone) or from My availability Time zone in the app.
Set your time zone before creating weekly patterns. Your weekly hours are read in this time zone. If you change it later, Mashora automatically rebuilds your upcoming open slots and days off in the new zone — appointments that are already booked are never moved.
04
Step 4

Connect your payout account

Mashora pays you through Stripe Connect. You don't need an existing Stripe account — one is created for you during setup.

  1. In the app, open Profile Payments — or the Payouts card on the dashboard's Profile page.
  2. Tap Connect bank account.
  3. Complete Stripe's secure form. Your banking details go directly to Stripe — never to Mashora.
  4. Return to the app. Your status updates automatically.

Step 5

Publish your availability

Choose when patients can book you — from the app's Availability tab or the dashboard's Availability page. Mix and match all three.

Weekly patterns

Pick a day, start and end time, and a session length of 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Slots are generated four weeks ahead and keep rolling. Pause or resume a pattern any time.

One-off slots

Add a single slot on a specific date and time — perfect for extra hours outside your usual week.

Days off

Block a whole day. Open slots on it are removed straight away, and your weekly patterns skip it. Booked appointments are never touched.

Mashora automatically leaves a 15-minute gap between consecutive sessions, so a consultation that runs over never collides with the next one. Patients always see your slots in their own time zone.

Step 6

Manage your bookings

Every confirmed booking reaches you instantly, with everything you need to prepare.

  • A notification for every new confirmed booking and every cancellation.
  • Open a booking to read the patient's note and view any files they uploaded — lab results, scans or X-rays.
  • Chat with the patient in the booking — it stays open until 7 days after the appointment.
  • A reminder 30 minutes before every appointment.

Step 7

Run the consultation

Consultations happen in a private video room inside the Mashora app.

  1. At the start time, open the booking in the app and tap Join video call. The button unlocks exactly on time.
  2. Whoever joins first, the other is notified right away that the session has started.
  3. When you're done, tap End call. The session closes for both of you and the patient is invited to rate the visit.
  4. If nobody ends the call, the session completes on its own 15 minutes after its scheduled end.

Step 8

Write the medical report

Reports are written on the web dashboard, where there's room to type comfortably.

  1. In the dashboard, open Bookings the booking Report.
  2. Fill in the template: diagnosis, medications (name, dosage and duration), follow-up notes and an optional follow-up date.
  3. Submit. A PDF is generated and sent to your patient automatically, and they're notified right away.

Step 9

How you get paid

You set your fee, and you receive it in full for every completed session.

  • Paid after the session. Your fee is transferred to your Stripe account when the session completes — not when the patient books.
  • Late cancellations. If a patient cancels less than 24 hours before, you receive 30% of what they paid, straight away.
  • Need to cancel? Contact our support team — the patient receives a full refund and there's no payout for that booking.
  • To your bank. Stripe then pays your balance out to your bank account on your Stripe payout schedule.

FAQ

Doctor questions

Can I accept bookings before I'm approved?

No. Your profile becomes visible to patients, and you can publish availability, once our team has approved your CV and specialty certificate.

Can I change my fee?

Yes, any time, from the app or the dashboard. The new fee applies to new bookings — existing bookings keep the price they were made at.

What happens if a patient doesn't join?

The session closes automatically 15 minutes after its scheduled end and is treated as completed — including your payout.

What if I need to cancel an appointment?

Contact our support team from the dashboard or the app. We'll cancel the booking and fully refund your patient, who is notified automatically.

Can I hold video calls from the dashboard?

Video calls take place in the Mashora app. The dashboard is for bookings, reports, availability and your profile settings.

My patients are in other countries. Which time do they see?

Their own. You set your hours in your time zone, and every patient sees your slots converted to the local time of their phone.

Do I need my own Stripe account?

No. When you tap “Connect bank account”, Stripe creates your payout account for you as part of the setup form.
