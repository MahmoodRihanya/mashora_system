FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Mashora for medical emergencies?

No. Mashora is for scheduled consultations. If you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department right away.

How much does a consultation cost?

Each doctor sets their own fee. The price you see on the doctor's profile is the full price you pay, in US dollars — nothing is added at checkout.

What do I need for a video consultation?

Just the Mashora app on your phone, a camera and microphone, and a stable internet connection. The call happens inside the app — there's nothing else to install.

What if my connection drops during the call?

Simply tap “Join” again. The call stays open from your start time until 15 minutes after the scheduled end, so you can rejoin as many times as you need.

Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes, from the booking in the app. Cancel 24 hours or more before your visit for a full refund. Within 24 hours you get 50% back — the rest compensates your doctor for the reserved time and covers the platform fee.

How are doctors verified?

Every doctor uploads a CV and a specialty certificate. Our team reviews both before the doctor can publish availability or accept bookings. You can read a doctor's CV right from their profile.

My doctor is in another country. Which time will I see?

Always your own. Doctors set their time zone, and the app shows every appointment in the local time of the phone you're using — for you and for your doctor.
